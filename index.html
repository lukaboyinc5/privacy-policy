1. About this policy

Tasbeeqa (package name com.lukaboy.tasbeeqa) is a tasbeeh and dhikr counter for Android, published by [DEVELOPER NAME]. This policy explains what the app does with information. It applies to the app only.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The developer does not receive your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage data. The app has no sign-up, no login and no internet access, so nothing can be sent from the app to the developer or to anyone else.

3. Data stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following information in its private storage on your device only:

DataPurpose
Dhikr list, including any custom dhikr you add (name, Arabic text, target)To show and count your dhikr
Current count and daily countsTo show your counter, daily total, 7-day chart, streak and lifetime counts
Settings (theme, haptic feedback, volume button counting, keep screen on, default target)To remember your choices

This data is not uploaded, not backed up to any cloud service by the app, and not readable by other apps under normal Android security rules. App backup is turned off.

4. Permissions

Tasbeeqa requests no Android permissions. In particular:

  • Haptic feedback uses the standard system touch feedback, so no vibration permission is needed.
  • Counting with the volume buttons works only while the app is open and in the foreground.
  • Keeping the screen on uses a window setting that needs no permission.
  • The app does not access your files, camera, microphone, contacts, location or network.

5. Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social media code, and it does not share data with any third party. The Arabic text font (Amiri) is bundled inside the app and licensed under the SIL Open Font License. It is not downloaded from the internet.

6. Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children either. The app contains no ads, purchases or links that lead outside the app.

7. Your control and deletion

You are in control of all data in the app. You can reset a counter or delete a custom dhikr inside the app. To remove everything, clear the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Tasbeeqa → Storage, or uninstall the app. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing to request or delete from us.

8. Security

Since data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security, such as the screen lock and Android app sandboxing. Please keep your device updated and locked.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Any new version of the app that changes how data is handled will be reflected here before release.
